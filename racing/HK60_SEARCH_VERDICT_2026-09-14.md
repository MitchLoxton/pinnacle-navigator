# HK60 SEARCH VERDICT — 2026-09-14

## User objective
Find a Hong Kong system around 60 bets/year with strong return and controlled drawdown that is defensible enough to follow.

## Decision
**NO ~60-BET SYSTEM IS PROMOTED TODAY. DO NOT FORCE THE FREQUENCY TARGET.**

The search produced historically attractive ~53-60 bet/year rules, but when rules were frozen before later-data checks, the higher-frequency R3 expansions failed badly in 2026. A separate V4 + HK VALUE add-on portfolio reached ~59 bets/year historically, but the incremental no-rebate VALUE add-on had negative calibrated model EV after removing V4 overlaps, so it is not a defensible way to inflate bet count.

### HK60 V1 — rejected
- Development: ~60.71 bets/year, ~44.46% stressed ROI, ~A$85k DD, zero losing development years.
- Reused audit fell to 40 bets/year and introduced two losing years.
- 2026 source-cutoff diagnostic: ~-A$96.8k at the A$5k research scale, ~-50.9% realized ROI.

### HK60 V2 — rejected
- 2015-2025: 57.64 bets/year, ~A$107.3k annual historical P/L, ~37.23% ROI, ~A$100k chronological DD, zero losing completed years.
- Frozen before its 2026 diagnostic.
- 2026 source cutoff: **-A$106.8k**, **-53.38% ROI**, ~A$110k DD despite positive model EV.

### HK55 R3 SIMPLE V1 — rejected
- 2015-2025: 53.27 bets/year, ~33.8% ROI, ~A$114.5k DD, zero losing completed years.
- 2026 source cutoff: **-A$84.5k**, **-41.2% ROI**, ~A$125k DD.

### V4 + HK VALUE frequency portfolio — not promoted
At a small VALUE add-on research scale it reached about 59 bets/year with attractive realized historical portfolio metrics, but the non-overlapping VALUE add-on selections had negative aggregate no-rebate calibrated model EV. That means the apparent frequency/return improvement is not sufficiently model-supported and must not be promoted simply because realized history looked good.

## Current best decision
Keep the existing Hong Kong production/reliability lock. The higher-frequency target is subordinate to edge quality. Current frozen lower-frequency candidates remain research/shadow evidence; no serious real-money Hong Kong system is production-approved yet.

## What would justify a future ~60/year production candidate
Do not do another same-history threshold grid. The next candidate must add genuinely independent information or execution evidence, for example:
- a separately validated PLACE sleeve using real time-stamped executable PLACE quotes rather than synthetic historical PLACE prices; or
- a new runner-level model frozen before later races and validated prospectively.

Any future promotion still requires fresh forward calibration, real executable-price/fill evidence, capacity evidence, and risk/drawdown behavior consistent with predeclared limits.
