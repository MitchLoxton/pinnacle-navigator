HOW THE LIVE BET + $3 STATISTICS RULE WORK
TWO DIFFERENT PRICE CHECKS
LIVE PRICE → BET DECISION · POST-RACE PRICE → $3+ EVIDENCE
DO NOT MIX THESE UP: A race can be a valid live V11 lock when the available fixed price passes the live gate, then later close below $3.00 and be EXCLUDED from the workbook's $3+ ROI evidence. The race result still updates the loss-state sequence.
1. Live market: TABtouch runner prices and the fixed-odds favourite refresh automatically.
2. Decision window: inside 30 minutes of the scheduled jump, V11 automatically recalculates the final stake while there is still more than 60 seconds to the start.
3. Live BET LOCKED: if the live favourite/price qualifies and the frozen V11 calculation returns a stake above A$0, the horse, stake, lock price and minimum executable price are saved for that race.
4. Execution price guard: after a lock, the locked horse is re-priced every 15 seconds. Current price below MIN EXEC = DO NOT PLACE; current price at/above MIN EXEC = BET NOW.
5. Final live NO BET: if no qualifying lock exists by the 60-second safety buffer, the race becomes FINAL NO BET.
6. Post-race $3 evidence gate: after the race, the saved TAB.com.au final fixed-WIN closing price is checked separately. $3.00+ = eligible for exact-state ROI evidence. Below $3.00 = excluded from exact-state ROI evidence. Either way, the race still updates the loss-state sequence.
7. Results: TODAY'S RESULTS shows the live V11 lock outcome separately from the $3+ statistics/evidence status so a live wager is never confused with a historical-evidence qualifier.
8. App updates: the race-day client checks for a newer build every 15 seconds and reloads itself so an old screen cannot keep showing stale instructions.
