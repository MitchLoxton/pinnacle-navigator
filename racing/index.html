WHAT IS THE FINAL TRIGGER?
FINAL TRIGGER
V11 FINAL STAKE > A$0 NEAR THE JUMP
1. Favourite: the current market favourite must be known.
2. Near the jump: V11 uses the best fixed price you can actually obtain at that time. An earlier price is only a preview.
3. Price gate: the live price must be at least $3.00. Passing $3.00 does not create the bet by itself.
4. V11 calculation: the engine checks the race + current loss-state/evidence, then applies the V11 stake and risk controls.
5. The actual trigger: if that calculation finishes with FINAL STAKE = A$0, it is NO BET. If it finishes with FINAL STAKE > A$0, the engine creates BET LOCKED and the app turns green with the horse, stake and minimum odds.
Live market: each watch card reads the public TABtouch fixed-WIN market and refreshes automatically every 15 seconds. Scratched runners are removed and the fixed-odds favourite is identified from TABtouch's live race data.
