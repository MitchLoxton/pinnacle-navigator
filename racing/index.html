LATE-CONFIRMATION SAFETY MODE
MONITOR EARLY · EXECUTE LATE
NO REAL BET SIGNAL UNTIL 45–10s BEFORE JUMP
WHY: an early $3+ quote can shorten before the jump. V11 now monitors earlier prices but refuses to create a real BET LOCKED instruction until the final execution window. This reduces the chance of betting a horse that later goes too short. It cannot guarantee the eventual Official SP, because Official SP is only known after the market closes.
1. Early monitoring: TABtouch odds and the favourite can be watched from 30 minutes out, but this is NOT permission to bet.
2. Final execution window: a real V11 decision can only lock from 45 seconds down to 10 seconds before the scheduled jump.
3. Live price gate: the current verified favourite must still be at least $3.00 fixed WIN. The old 5% MIN EXEC tolerance has been removed; the workbook's live floor is $3.00.
4. Same-favourite guard: after a lock, the phone re-checks every 5 seconds. If the locked horse is no longer the single verified favourite, DO NOT PLACE.
5. Price guard: after a lock, if the current fixed-WIN price falls below $3.00, DO NOT PLACE. If the quote cannot be verified, DO NOT PLACE.
6. Too-late guard: with 5 seconds or less remaining, a new placement is blocked. Do not chase a disappearing market.
7. Final NO BET: if no qualifying late-confirmed V11 lock exists by 10 seconds before jump, the race becomes FINAL NO BET.
8. Post-race evidence: the workbook's $3 evidence rule uses Official SP. Final fixed-WIN closing odds are displayed only as a reference and are not automatically used as Official SP.
