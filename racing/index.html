HOW THE FINAL DECISION WORKS
AUTOMATIC V11 DECISION
WATCH → BET LOCKED / NO BET → RESULT
POST-LOCK EXECUTION GUARD: BET LOCKED is only permission to act while the locked horse's current fixed-WIN price is still at or above the saved MIN EXEC. If the price drops below MIN EXEC, the big box changes to DO NOT PLACE — PRICE TOO LOW. The app keeps checking every 15 seconds and can return to BET NOW if the price recovers before the race.
1. Live market: TABtouch runner prices and the fixed-odds favourite refresh automatically.
2. Decision window: inside 30 minutes of the scheduled jump, V11 automatically recalculates the final stake while there is still more than 60 seconds to the start.
3. BET LOCKED: if the frozen V11 calculation returns a stake above A$0, the horse, stake, lock price and minimum executable price are saved permanently for that race.
4. Execution price guard: after a lock, the locked horse is re-priced every 15 seconds. Current price below MIN EXEC = DO NOT PLACE; current price at/above MIN EXEC = BET NOW.
5. NO BET: if no qualifying lock exists by the 60-second safety buffer, the race becomes FINAL NO BET. It no longer waits forever.
6. After the race: the race leaves COMING UP and moves to TODAY'S RESULTS, showing the winner, V11 result, and the final TABtouch fixed-WIN closing favourite/odds.
7. App updates: the race-day client checks for a newer build every 15 seconds and reloads itself so an old screen cannot keep showing WAIT after the server has locked a decision.
