IMPORTANT: the app now keeps three things separate: (1) V11 model signal, (2) actual bookmaker execution, and (3) post-race Official-SP evidence. A model signal alone is never counted as a real betting loss.
1. Monitoring: early odds are information only. They cannot create an actual recorded wager.
2. Executable signal: V11 can become SIGNAL READY only from 20 seconds down to 10 seconds before the scheduled jump.
3. Price + favourite: it must still be the single verified favourite and at least $3.00 fixed WIN.
4. Actual wager: after the bookmaker accepts it, tap RECORD ACCEPTED BET and enter the accepted stake and price. The server rejects a V11 accepted price below $3.00 or a stake above the model request.
5. No confirmation = no cash result: if there is no accepted execution record, the race cannot be counted as an actual betting WIN/LOSS.
6. State sequence: the favourite's race result still updates the stream loss state whether you bet or not.
7. Historical evidence: exact-state N/ROI is updated only from a verified Official SP of $3.00+. Final fixed-WIN closing odds are reference only.
8. Fail closed: favourite changes, price below $3, unverified price, lost signal, or the 10-second cutoff = DO NOT PLACE.
