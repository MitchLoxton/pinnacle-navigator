76.1 mm OD — SECOND RAIL COMPOUND WRAP TEMPLATE

FOR TWO 76.1 mm RAILS MEETING THE SAME 76.1 mm UPRIGHT AT 90° IN PLAN · SAME HEIGHT

1 · RAIL A FIRST
Normal fishmouth Rail A to the upright and tack it in the exact finished position.
2 · WRAP RAIL B
Wrap this sheet around Rail B. Join the two seam edges exactly.
3 · AIM THE MARK
The bold centreline marked FACE RAIL A must point directly at Rail A.
4 · CUT + TEST
Trace the thick curve, remove the end beyond it, deburr, then test-fit before welding.
IMPORTANT: This is not two ordinary identical fishmouths. It is the compound cut for the second rail after the first rail is already fitted. It removes the Rail-A-side horn that otherwise collides. Use only if all three outside diameters are actually 76.1 mm and the two rail centre-lines are 90° apart in plan. Wall thickness, weld prep and real fabrication tolerances can require a small inside-edge dress. Make one test piece before any repeat cutting.
