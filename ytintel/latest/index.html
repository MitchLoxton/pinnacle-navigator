V0.9 · CURRENT · 4 SEP 2026

Specifics over AI water

LIVE
  • Why It Worked now needs receipts: hook, payoff cadence, re-hook and replay claims show the exact transcript example beside the number.
  • Hook framework upgrade: importance/stakes, proof-first, curiosity gap, challenge/experiment, problem, direct promise/how-to, contrarian and urgency categories with exact opening lines.
  • Clean list/detail extraction: list-style videos can show the actual details in a low-clutter numbered section with timestamps.
  • Dropped the confusing Promise first 60s / Promise full video percentage cards.
  • Pattern Mine rebuilt: now requires 5–10 near-identical videos and outputs master list → most repeated → unique → winner vs pack → hooks side-by-side → small stats table.
  • Pattern Mine includes a single Copy as Markdown output for Claude/ChatGPT/script workflows.
  • Similar Videos adds a public Replay Peak column when Most Replayed evidence is available; private Studio retention is not claimed.
  • Visual + Audio Vault now aggregates saved hotzone drivers and attributed audio across analysis history.
