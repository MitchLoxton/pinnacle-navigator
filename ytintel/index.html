V0.8 · CURRENT · 4 SEP 2026

Evidence-first analyser upgrade

LIVE
  • Rebuilt Hook Teardown into Hook → Re-hook → Promise Fulfillment with timestamped transcript evidence.
  • Added a dedicated Outlier Multiplier section comparing the video with the creator's recent long-form channel median.
  • Kept the real 100-point Most Replayed heatmap front-and-centre when YouTube exposes it; YTIntel does not fabricate missing replay data.
  • Added Copy full transcript as Markdown for sending clean timestamped transcripts to another AI or workflow.
  • Moved the concise video summary to the bottom of Analyse and made it transcript-derived, with actual methods for instructional videos and no forced fake method list for entertainment.
  • Removed the repetitive generic Do Not Copy block.
  • Added Visuals + Edit BETA: replay-hotzone targeting, hotzone transcript context, content-driver tags and any YouTube-attributed audio that is available.
  • Preserved/migrated existing v0.7 browser history into v0.8.
