# YTIntel Premium Single-Link Standard — v0.36

Source benchmark: Blake Ryan, **“YouTube's New Content Format… Here's How I'd Exploit It”** (6 Sep 2026 analysis). Product basis: **YTIntel: the endgame (brief)** and **Analyse one link: what you see, in order**.

This is a quality contract, not a content template. New analyses must match the benchmark's specificity, chronology, evidence discipline and useful depth. They must never copy Blake-specific facts, numbers, claims, conclusions, GTA/Nooky recommendations or wording into another source.

## Product promise

One YouTube link goes in. The creator should be able to understand the whole video without watching it, see why its package and retention mechanics may have worked, verify or quarantine important claims, understand visuals/audio only to the limit of available evidence, and leave with an original video plan tailored to Creator DNA. The vault must compound what is learned.

## The 18 screens, in order

1. **The strip** — title, channel, subscribers, views, likes, comments, upload date and age, length, outlier multiple against channel median with an age flag, niche/subniche and pull time.
2. **The summary — so you never need to watch it** — 130–260 word synopsis plus 10–15 chronological beat lines spanning the entire runtime and a cautious performance verdict.
3. **Key takeaways** — 5–8 distinct lessons, or the complete declared list when evidenced; each has mechanism/significance, application and exact receipt.
4. **The hook** — first 60 seconds line by line; every available caption segment gets a specific job; hook type, opening line and first promise are explicit.
5. **The re-hooks** — every distinct attention reset with time, receipt, why it resets attention and median spacing when measurable.
6. **The payoffs** — what pays the earlier promises, first payoff, payoffs/minute and title-versus-delivery verdict. Revenue claims are not proof by default.
7. **Most Replayed** — public curve, peaks and troughs with exact nearby transcript and verified frame when available. If no curve exists, say why the public pull is insufficient and show labelled transcript hypotheses only.
8. **The structure map** — every major beat with start/end, duration, function, what is said and what is on screen if a verified sample exists.
9. **Visuals — key frame by key frame** — production mode, thumbnail read, verified sampled frames, visual rhythm and replay-linked frame evidence. Never invent unseen frames.
10. **Motion graphics** — visible text overlays and sampled candidate changes. Stills do not prove continuous zooms, transitions, cuts or animation.
11. **Audio** — WPM overall and by minute, longest caption gap, public music credits and acoustic limits. No raw audio means no invented music/SFX/silence claims.
12. **Packaging** — title observations, likely title discrepancy/test signal without overclaiming, thumbnail composition when visually evidenced, title/hook keywords and promise versus delivery.
13. **The numbers table** — words, WPM, longest gap, beat count, median/longest beat, first receipt, first payoff, first sell, share selling and cuts/minute when actually measured. Every value names its measurement basis.
14. **The claims ledger** — up to 20 consequential numerical/factual claims with verdict, explanation, exact source receipt and external sources when independently verified.
15. **Channel context** — channel median, this video against it with upload-age caution, recent real hits, same-angle pattern mine and a cross-channel validation sample when public discovery supports it.
16. **Make it yours** — 2–3 materially different brand-aware directions, each with title variants, spoken hook, 5–10 beat plan, executable thumbnail, source receipts, steal/do-not-copy guidance and a measurement plan.
17. **The vault entry** — file the winner by the analysed video's niche/subniche, script/edit style, motion evidence, hook/title keywords and thumbnail composition tags; keep Creator DNA separate.
18. **Export** — Markdown/PDF controls, full transcript evidence appendix, research provenance, model state and Method + limits.

## Evidence rules

- Nothing factual prints without a source line, measured public field, verified image sample or external source.
- Timestamps inherit their actual precision. Caption-start timing is not word timing.
- Most Replayed is replay/seek interest, not audience retention.
- Missing media stays missing. A public thumbnail is not a frame-by-frame pass.
- Different public titles can mean edit, cache lag or a test. Only creator-authorised experiment data confirms an A/B test.
- Channel cumulative views need an age flag. A young upload is not automatically a flop because its current multiple is below 1.0.
- Private revenue, RPM and dashboard assertions remain **their claim** unless independently supported.
- Arithmetic may be checked independently of the premise that feeds it.
- Model explanations must synthesize. Transcript echo is a failure.

## Creator-transfer rules

Steal mechanisms: proof before persuasion, re-hooks, arithmetic done with the viewer, concrete physical mechanisms, objection handling, packaging tests, visual rhythm and other execution patterns supported by evidence.

Do not copy competitor wording, titles, thumbnail execution, personal stories, private numbers, unsupported claims, borrowed-IP assumptions or business-model risks. The final output is the video to make **inside the user's own channel, niche, audience and brand rules**.

## Acceptance gate

A release does not pass merely because 18 cards render. It passes when:

- all 18 sections are present and ordered;
- WPM is deduplicated and plausible;
- pull time and upload age are visible;
- channel median/outlier are computed or their absence explained;
- replay absence has a reason and hypothesis fallback;
- the first minute is line-function analysed;
- the whole runtime is structurally mapped;
- the claims ledger separates verified / creator claim / contradicted / arithmetic issue / uncertain;
- funnel/demo/CTA time is measured or clearly estimated;
- visual/audio claims stay inside the evidence boundary;
- the remake is Creator-DNA specific and has a post-publish measurement plan;
- exported Markdown/PDF preserves the same report and Method + limits.
